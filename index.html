Iyo Numä

Writer. Author. Screenplay Writer and Founder of NaRye Studios.

Iyo Numä is a writer, author, screenplay writer, and founder of NaRye Studios. She creates psychologically rich stories that explore identity, fear, resilience, transformation, and the unseen forces that shape us. Her work moves across psychological horror, Southern folklore, supernatural fiction, drama, and other forms of original storytelling. Through novels, screenplays, and developing intellectual properties, Iyo Numä creates stories where the familiar and the supernatural meet—and where whispered stories are finally spoken.

The Book

The Boo Hag’s Mirror

A Gullah Geechee Tale

The Boo Hag’s Mirror is an autobiographical psychological horror reimagining of Gullah Geechee folklore. Through fractured narratives, the book moves between a Southern family, a college campus, a nurse and a writer whose research into the legend becomes an intimate, dangerous encounter.

This is a book about what happens when folklore stops being story and becomes presence.
