About Iyo Numä

Who I Am

Iyo Numä is a writer, storyteller, and founder of NaRye Studios. Her work explores the unseen forces that shape human behavior, identity, memory, and transformation.

Drawing from Southern influences, African American culture, folklore traditions, symbolism, and psychology, she creates stories where tension lingers long after the story ends.

About NaRye Studios

A narrative-driven creative studio.

NaRye Studios develops original stories across publishing, film, television, and interactive media. The studio centers psychological horror, drama, folklore, and emotionally immersive storytelling.

Every property begins with character, atmosphere, and the question beneath the question: what happens when something familiar stops feeling safe?
