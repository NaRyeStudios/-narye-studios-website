Founder / Writer

Iyo Numä

Writer and founder of NaRye Studios, creating psychologically immersive folklore-rooted narratives that explore the unseen forces shaping human behavior.

Hi, I'm Iyo Numä, an American writer and the founder of NaRye Studios. I was raised in East Alabama, and my work is deeply shaped by my Southern upbringing, African American culture, and spiritual beliefs.

My writing often explores emotional realism, human behavior, and the unseen forces that shape everyday experiences. I am a self-taught conceptual storyteller, developing narratives from feeling and observation rather than traditional structure.

My work moves across psychological horror, drama, and introspective storytelling, with a strong emphasis on atmosphere, tension, and internal experience.

Much of my work exists within the realm of folklore with psychological horror undertones, which is why I identify as a Psychological Horror Folklore Writer.

“I'm not here to scare you. I'm here to show you something you already recognize.”
