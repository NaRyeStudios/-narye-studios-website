About NaRye Studios

A narrative-driven studio developing original stories across psychological horror, folklore, and drama.

NaRye Studios is a narrative-driven IP development studio. We develop original stories and creative properties across psychological horror, folklore, drama, and other narrative forms.

Our work is psychologically immersive, rooted in folklore and emotional realism, and focused on the unseen forces that shape human behavior.

NaRye Studios is the studio. Iyo Numä is the writer and founder whose voice runs through it.
