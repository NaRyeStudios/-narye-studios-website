Work

A selection of psychologically immersive folklore and narrative work. Each story explores the psyche under pressure—where perception, desire, and consequence begin to distort.

01

The Final Take

Psychological Supernatural Horror · Feature

Logline

An ambitious actress lands the role of a lifetime, only to realize the director is manipulating her reality to capture authentic fear—forcing her to become the character both on and off camera.

Description

At first, the role feels like a breakthrough. Then the boundaries blur. Then they disappear entirely. As filming intensifies, scenes begin reflecting her private thoughts, memories, and fears—things she never shared. The director insists it’s all part of the process. But when she uncovers footage of past actresses who didn’t leave the production unchanged, she realizes the performance was never meant to end.

02

Black Bonds

Psychological Folk Horror · Feature

Logline

A woman casts a love spell to secure her relationship—only to realize she hasn’t summoned devotion, but something that refuses to release her.

Description

What begins as control turns into possession, as her partner’s behavior becomes increasingly unnatural. As unseen rules begin to surface, she must confront whether she bound him—or bound herself to something older than desire.

03

Becoming Her

Psychological Thriller / Dark Drama · Feature

Logline

A socially isolated woman becomes obsessed with reinventing herself after encountering a charismatic public figure—but her transformation begins to demand more than change.

Description

As she sheds her old identity, she starts removing the people who remind her of it. Each step forward requires something to be left behind. The question becomes whether she’s evolving… or erasing herself completely.

04

Say It Again

Psychological Supernatural Horror · Feature

Logline

Grieving her father’s death, a young woman connects with a voice that claims to be him—but it knows things it shouldn’t… and refuses to be ignored.

Description

What begins as comfort quickly becomes control. The voice grows more specific, more invasive—guiding her decisions, isolating her from others. As her dependence deepens, she must confront whether she’s speaking to her father… or something that learned how to sound like him.

05

The Continuum

Psychological / Meta Horror · Feature

Logline

While watching a film alone, a woman realizes the background details are changing—and something inside the movie has started noticing her.

Description

Scenes begin reflecting fragments of her life—subtle at first, then undeniable. The line between viewer and subject dissolves as the film adjusts itself around her. The horror isn’t what’s in the movie—but why it’s responding to her at all.
